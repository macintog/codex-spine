This Codex skill is an `adapt-pattern-only` derivative of two
skills from the pstack plugin in the Cursor plugins repository:

- Project: <https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/60c641e4fad674784b30abcf9f8915dea39df38d/pstack>
- License at reviewed commit: <https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/60c641e4fad674784b30abcf9f8915dea39df38d/pstack/LICENSE>
- Pinned commit: `60c641e4fad674784b30abcf9f8915dea39df38d`
- Reviewed sources: `pstack/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`,
  `pstack/skills/technical-writing/SKILL.md`, `pstack/README.md`,
  `pstack/.cursor-plugin/plugin.json`, and `pstack/LICENSE`
- License: MIT

The adaptation retains the prose-pattern catalog and selected technical-writing
rules. It removes pstack's always-on and explicit-only Cursor routing,
product-specific commands, blanket punctuation bans, and dependence on other
pstack skills. The Codex-native packet narrows automatic routing to
consequential prose and protects literal evidence from stylistic rewriting.

The upstream technical-writing file credits Diataxis, Google developer style,
ASD-STE100 Issue 9, and *The Global English Style Guide*. This packet adapts the pinned MIT-licensed pstack text into general writing
principles. It does not redistribute those books, standards, dictionaries,
source pages, or their worked examples, and does not claim certification or
endorsement by their authors. The full upstream MIT notice is retained below.
